Why one tempo

When the time from takeaway to impact never changes, the only variable left is how far the putter goes back. Distance control becomes a question of length, which is far easier to repeat than a sense of hit.

How the length is worked out

The Stimp speed gives the green's rolling friction. From that the app gets the ball speed needed for the distance, divides by the putter's energy transfer (about 1.3) and finds the backstroke that reaches that speed in the forward-stroke time.

How to practise

Start with the ladder at 1, 3 and 6 metres. Let the tic start the putter moving and let the toc arrive as the ball is struck. Watch the ruler marks to learn what each length feels like.
